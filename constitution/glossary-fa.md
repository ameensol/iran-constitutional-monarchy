## Translation Conventions

- **Code stays English**: Solidity keywords, contract names (`Parliament.sol`), function names (`amendParameter()`), parameter names (`PARAM_CROWN_LAW_DEADLINE`), and variable names remain in English.
- **Article references**: Constitutional article references (اصل I.1, اصل II.4, etc.) use اصل with the original numbering scheme.
- **Part numbering**: Parts use Persian ordinals (بخش یکم, بخش دوم, etc.) in headings.
- **Loanwords**: Established loanwords preferred where Persian neologisms would be unclear (بلاکچین, پارامتر, وتو).
- **1906 Constitution vocabulary**: Where applicable, terms from Iran's 1906 مشروطه Constitution are used (انجمن ایالتی, مجلس شورای ملی, اصل for article).
- **Register**: Formal constitutional Persian, third person, declarative. Modeled on the 1906 Supplementary Fundamental Law (متمم قانون اساسی مشروطه) drafting style.
- **Numbers**: Persian numerals (۱, ۲, ۳) used in body text; Arabic numerals in article numbering (I.1, II.4) for consistency with the English original.
- **Quotation marks**: Persian guillemets (« ») for translated quotations; standard quotes (" ") in English code references.
